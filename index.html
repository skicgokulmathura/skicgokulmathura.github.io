About Our School

Established in the historic land of Gokul, Mathura, Shri Krishna Inter College stands as a pillar of educational excellence and discipline. Catering to students from Class 6th through 12th, the college provides comprehensive academic learning across Science, Arts, and Commerce streams. Grounded in strong cultural heritage and dedicated to holistic development, our institution empowers young minds with modern knowledge, ethical values, and leadership qualities through academics, sports and NCC for a bright future.

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

NCC
Shri Krishna Inter College proudly runs an NCC unit to build discipline, courage, teamwork and a spirit of service among our students. Through regular parade, drill and training, cadets learn leadership and love for the nation, preparing them for a responsible future and for careers in the Army, Navy, Air Force and police services.
